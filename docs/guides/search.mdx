# Search (⌘K)

Press **⌘K** (`Ctrl+K`) or click **Search…** in the top bar. Type a name or a
few words, then press **↵** to open the result. **Esc** closes it.

## What it finds

- **Things in the workspace:**
  - issues, projects, pages, agents, crews, skills, credentials, routines,
    people and integrations
  - missions (they open on their timeline)
  - routine schedules and automations (they open in the routine's Plan view;
    a schedule scrolls to its row)
  - crew tools (they open on that server in Integrations › Crew tools)
  - conversations, matched against the message text
- **Every card in Settings.** Examples: *Danger zone*, *Sessions & access*,
  *Who may reveal*, *Pages appearance*. Settings opens on the card's tab,
  scrolls to the card and marks it for a moment. You can also link to a
  card yourself with `?card=`, for example `/settings?tab=general&card=danger-zone`.
- **Tabs and sections inside pages:**
  - Integrations (notification connections, incoming webhooks, app catalog,
    connected accounts, MCP endpoints and the rest)
  - Inbox views, Activity sections, Journal tabs, the routine calendar,
    missions and model providers
- **Actions that sit behind a button:** *Create issue*, *Invite member*,
  *Create CLI token*, *Change password*, *Store missing avatars*, plus
  *Create new agent* and *Create new crew*.
- **The Admin console**, for instance administrators: runtime, limits, data
  retention, people & workspaces, security sections and backups.
- **Other workspaces you belong to.** Pick one to switch to it.

Cards, sections, actions and workspaces show only once you start typing. An
empty search box shows your recent results, quick actions and the main pages.

## How it matches

- A result whose name starts with what you typed ranks first. Next come
  names where a word starts with it, then names that contain it anywhere.
- Several words match in any order: `avatars store` finds *Store missing
  avatars*, and `general danger` finds the Danger zone card.
- Each row carries the words people use for it. For example, `slack` finds
  *Notification connections* and `cost` finds *Spend*.
- On the right, each row says where it lives, such as *Settings › General*.

## Who sees what

Search shows only what you can open. Every list comes from the API,
filtered for your role. Each card, section and action is checked with the
same rule as the page that holds it:

| What | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Danger zone | the workspace owner |
| Invite member, Store missing avatars, Spend, Audit log | ADMIN and OWNER |
| Access & Secrets cards | MANAGER and above |
| Admin console | instance administrators |
