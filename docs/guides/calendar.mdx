# Calendar

`/calendar` shows dated work across the workspace. The calendar has no events of
its own: each date belongs to something that already exists, changes there, and
shows only to people who can open it.

## What it shows

| Layer | Source | Kinds |
|---|---|---|
| Routine starts and runs | the routine calendar (`GET /api/v1/workspaces/{ws}/pipelines/calendar`) | the next starts of each enabled repeating schedule, one-time starts waiting in the queue, and recorded runs |

Issue due dates, milestones and decision deadlines are not on the calendar yet;
they join when the API serves them by date range.

A date is shown only when its routine is in your routine list. Times are in your
browser's time zone; a schedule that runs in another zone shows both times in
its peek.

## Views

- **Week** (default on a desktop) — the seven days with only the hours that have
  something in them, and a line at the current time this week. A busy hour
  shows three items and `+N`, which opens that day's agenda.
- **Agenda** (default on a phone) — the next two weeks from the chosen day,
  Today and Tomorrow first; empty days are left out, and a routine with many
  planned starts in a day reads as one row (`×N`, first–last time).
- **Month** — whole weeks; three items a day, `+N more`, and a red dot on a day
  where a run could not finish.

`‹ Today ›` moves by the view. The view and the day are in the URL
(`/calendar?view=month&date=2026-10-15`), so a reload or a shared link opens the
same page.

## Panel

- **Views** — **Mine** (routines you published) or **Workspace**; keys `1` and
  `2`. The choice is remembered for you (`calendar.view` preference).
- **Crews** — narrows to the routines a crew authored.
- **Filter › Status** — At risk (a run that could not finish), Waiting for
  someone, Planned, Done. Nothing checked means everything; active filters show
  as chips you can remove.
- **Search** (`/`) looks through the dates already loaded.

Above the views, **Need you soon** lists up to four runs that wait for a person
or failed today.

## Peek and actions

Click an item to open its peek (`J` / `K` move, `Esc` closes): when it is, why
you see it, and where the date comes from.

| Item | Main action | Also |
|---|---|---|
| Planned start of a repeating schedule | Open routine | **Run now** (anyone who may run routines) |
| One-time start | Open routine | **Cancel start** (Manager and above), with a confirmation |
| Run | Open run — **Decide** when it waits for a person and you are Manager or above | — |

## Add a date

**Add a date › A routine start** (or a click on an empty hour in Week) opens
**Schedule a start**:

- **One time** — a day and a time; the start joins the routine's queue
  (`POST …/pipelines/{slug}/run` with `fire_at`) and uses the published version.
- **Repeating** — a schedule with presets or a cron expression and a time zone;
  the next three starts come from the server
  (`GET …/pipeline-schedules/preview`). Only Owner, Admin, Manager or members
  with the `routine.create` capability can add one; everyone else sees why.

## From the command line

```bash
crewship routine calendar --from 2026-10-12T00:00:00Z --to 2026-10-19T00:00:00Z
```

Routines › Calendar keeps its own view and links to this page with
**Open in Calendar**.
