- **The sharing guides now say that workspace owners and admins can open every Design, including one that was never shared (#4791).** The Design guide said a Design without a folder is visible only to its creator until shared. Since #4628 workspace owners and admins can read and edit every Design, and each access to someone else's Design is written to the audit log as `design.workspace_admin_accessed`. *Sharing and access* and *Design* now describe this, how to list those entries with `crewship audit`, and that other artifact types keep their own rules.
