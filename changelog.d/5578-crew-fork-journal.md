- **`crewship journal verify` no longer fails on a crew restored with `backup restore --as-crew`.** A crew bundle carries only that crew's journal entries, with sequence numbers from the whole workspace's chain, and the restore kept the resulting gaps, so the copy's journal read as tampered immediately after the restore. A crew-scope fork now renumbers the extract into a contiguous chain, and the `backup.chain_resigned` entry records the renumbering and the source sequence range (#5578).
