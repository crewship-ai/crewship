- **An invited member of an existing workspace was sent into the "Set up Crewship" wizard on first sign-in.** Onboarding status is per user, so anyone added to a workspace someone else had set up counted as "not onboarded", and the status read could even create the Crewship Guide crew in that workspace on their behalf. `GET /api/v1/onboarding/status` (and `crewship system onboarding status`) now reports `completed: true` for a user who belongs only to workspaces they do not own, and creates nothing for them; the dashboard also stays put for anyone whose role in the current workspace is not `OWNER`. Owners and people with no workspace keep the wizard. (#4876)
