- **Docs History keeps editing sessions (#5236).** A Docs edit now pins its confirmed result as a checkpoint when the newest checkpoint is more than ten minutes old, or was written by another author at least a minute earlier, so History lists later revisions with their author and time instead of only "Revision 0". Session checkpoints are best effort and never refuse an edit. They share at most half of the History budget and are never deleted: when that half is used, no new automatic snapshots are kept, edits still save, and History says so (`history_limit_reached` in `crewship doc checkpoints`).
