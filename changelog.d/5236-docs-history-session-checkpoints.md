- **Docs History keeps editing sessions (#5236).** A Docs edit now pins its confirmed result as a checkpoint when the newest checkpoint is more than ten minutes old, or was written by another author at least a minute earlier, so History lists later revisions with their author and time instead of only "Revision 0". Session checkpoints are best effort and never refuse an edit; they share at most half of the History budget, and when it is full the oldest session checkpoints make room for the newest.
