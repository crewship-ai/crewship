- ⚠️ **Behaviour change: only a crew's managers publish its pages, and a public link pauses while its creator is suspended or gone (GHSA-pxcv-gc75-x68m).** Before, any member of the owning crew could publish a crew-owned page to the internet, whatever their crew role, and a public link kept opening after the person who created it was suspended or left the workspace. Now publishing a crew-owned page takes a MANAGER (or higher) of that crew, workspace role or crew override, or the crew's Manage grant, or a workspace admin. A link opens only while its creator is a member and not suspended; it is withheld, not revoked, and opens again if they return.
