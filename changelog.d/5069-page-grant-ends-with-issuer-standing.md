- ⚠️ **Behaviour change: a Page share no longer comes back when the person who issued it regains access.** A Page, comment or workbook editor grant ends when its issuer loses the authority to issue it, through a role change, removal, suspension, Guest access anywhere, leaving the owning crew or a Page ownership change. Restoring that access does not revive it, even with no read in between; the grants list keeps it as inactive and says it must be issued again, and issuing it again is a new journalled grant. On upgrade, grants whose issuer has no standing at that moment end the same way; live grants are untouched.
