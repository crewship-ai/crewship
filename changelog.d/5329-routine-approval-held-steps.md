- **A routine's `crewship` step on a crew whose autonomy level holds the action no longer fails with "a routine cannot wait for an approval".** It opens an approval card naming the action and its arguments and waits; approving runs that exact call (a crash after the write but before the step finished re-runs it on resume, as for any `crewship` step). The approval is bound to the run, foreach item, step, verb and arguments, so it never unlocks another item or changed arguments, and standing trust grants neither approve it nor are offered on its card. Only a trusted, unsuspended workspace OWNER or ADMIN may decide it, on every door; the public waitpoint callback cannot. Ordinary `wait: approval` steps are unchanged.
