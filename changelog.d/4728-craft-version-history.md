- **Version history for native Word documents and Sheets (#4728).** Behind the workspace flag `craft_version_history` (off by default), Artifacts lists every saved version of a native Word document or Sheet with its time and author, previews any version read-only, compares an older version with the current one line by line, and lets the person holding the editing lease restore it as a new version; nothing is deleted. Anyone who may read the document sees the history; comment-only access does not. Workspace owners and admins reading another member's history or versions are audited. New `GET /api/v1/pages/{slug}/craft/document/versions` and `.../versions/{version}`, with `crewship craft versions` and `crewship craft version <page> <version> [--output FILE]`.
