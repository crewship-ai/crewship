- **Each slide of a Slides artifact has its own link, and the deck viewer follows the keyboard (#5170, behind `artifact_slides`).** The address ends in `#slide-<id>` for the selected slide, so a copied link, a reload and the browser's Back and Forward buttons return to that slide. ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home and End move between slides in the viewer when focus is not in a field, menu or dialog, and the filmstrip, grid and notes keep the same selection. Closing **Present** with Esc or leaving full screen leaves the viewer on the slide you presented last.
