- **Design canvas: pan and zoom like a design tool (#5143).** A right, middle or Space + left drag and a two-finger trackpad scroll pan the canvas; Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or a pinch zooms around the pointer; **Fit** frames every layer. On touch, one finger on the background pans and a pinch zooms. A right drag no longer moves a layer, the browser menu is suppressed only inside the canvas, and Escape, a cancelled pointer or leaving the window put back what the gesture changed. The view is local to each person and is never saved; panning never changes layer coordinates.
