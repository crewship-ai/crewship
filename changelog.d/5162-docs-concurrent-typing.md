- **Typing in the same Docs paragraph as someone else keeps your characters in order (#5162).** When another person's change landed in the paragraph you were typing in, your caret could slip one character back and your next keystrokes went in front of your own text; both views then agreed on the scrambled text and the chip said Saved. Your caret now stays after the character you typed last, and the chip says Saved only while the server's confirmed copy holds everything in your editor; otherwise editing stops with your changes offered for download.
