- **Native Craft history shows who wrote an accepted agent proposal and who approved it (#5599).** A Word or Sheet proposal now names who decided it and when (`decided_by`, `decided_at`), and a version saved by accepting a proposal carries `proposal` with the proposal's id and its agent author, while the version's own author remains the person who accepted it. `crewship craft versions`, `crewship craft version` and the `connect` proposal actions print these fields. Only callers who can already read the version history see them.
