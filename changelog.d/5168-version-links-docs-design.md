- **Version links of Docs and Designs open in Artifacts (#5168).** An old `/pages/<slug>?version=N` link of a Doc or a Design now moves to `/artifacts/<id>?version=N` instead of staying on Pages with "Can't show this artifact here". A Doc opens with History showing revision N selected, or says the revision is not in History. A Design opens in its editor at the current version. A panel Page or a native Word, which have no versions, still stay on Pages.
