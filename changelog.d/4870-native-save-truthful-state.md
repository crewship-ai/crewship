- **A Word or Sheet save the server confirmed no longer reads as "Neuložený koncept" just because the browser could not remove its local copy (#4870, #4852).** When the server had committed exactly the editor's content but the local recovery copy could not be removed, the status said the work was unsaved. It now reads "Uloženo na serveru · místní kopie zatím zůstává v prohlížeči" until the copy is removed and the editor confirms the version; any newer edit still shows "Neuložený koncept". Two remaining ways to stop native saving are closed: a failed local clean-up after an unchanged autosave now retries once by itself, and a browser-storage step that never answers is cut off at the save deadline with a visible status instead of leaving saves and Ctrl+S waiting for ever. Nothing is sent twice and no newer local copy is removed.
