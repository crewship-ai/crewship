- **A chat lists the artifacts its person attached to its messages (#5317).** `GET /api/v1/chats/{chatId}/artifact-refs` answers the lasting link from a conversation to its Sheets, oldest first and 100 at a time. Each reference shows only while the caller may see that page, and a hidden one is neither listed nor counted. A deleted page drops out, and a chat the caller may not read answers 404. It is read-only and returns metadata, never content.
