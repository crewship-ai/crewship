- **Word and Sheet: a slow browser draft store no longer leaves you holding a document you cannot edit (#5104).** Taking over editing needs this browser's private draft store. The editor now tries to open it up to three times, each attempt with its own deadline. If it still fails, the editor gives the fresh lease back (fenced by its generation, so it cannot affect a later holder), says why editing did not start, and offers **Převzít úpravy** again. A store that answers only after the editor gave up is closed and never unlocks the editor.
